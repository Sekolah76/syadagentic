### Golden Windows for Indonesia
- **Weekday 11:00-13:00** — lunch break scrolling
- **Weekday 19:00-21:00** — tiduran scroll HP (bedtime browsing)
- **Minggu 19:00-21:00** — peak weekend engagement

### Why These Times Work
- Monday: slow start, people scroll during commute/settling in
- Tuesday: more active, earlier browsing
- Wednesday: busiest day, people feel mid-week pressure, highest activity before lunch
- Thursday: gearing up for weekend, active daytime + early evenings
- Friday: last work day, engagement spikes midday-afternoon
- Saturday: people sleep in, early morning + midday are sweet spots
- Sunday: late afternoon/evening after housework done

## 📝 Post Format That Works on Threads

### Optimal Length
- **50-200 kata (sweet spot)**
- Terlalu pendek (<10 kata) = kurang depth
- Terlalu panjang (>300) = males baca
- 2-4 paragraf pendek ideal

### Winning Post Structures
1. **Hook + Story + CTA** — most common viral format
2. **Hot take / unpopular opinion** — drives comments
3. **"Am I the only one yang..."** — relatability hook
4. **Listicle** — "5 hal yang gw pelajari dari..."
5. **Storytime** — pengalaman pribadi
6. **Observation** — relatable daily life takes
7. **Before/After narrative** — great for product reviews

### Viral Post Traits
- Opening hook dalam 1 kalimat pertama
- Relatable human experience
- Ends with pertanyaan atau challenge
- 2-3 emoji max (💀😂😭🫠✨💅🔥)
- Short paragraphs (1-3 sentences each)
- Conversational tone throughout

## 🎯 Affiliate Content Strategy (Threads)

### Format yang Works (SOFT SELL)
- **Storytime approach:** "gara2 [masalah], gue akhirnya nyoba [produk]. results? 👇"
- **Relatable hook:** "siapa yang punya masalah [pain point]? gue dulu jg gitu..."
- **Honest review:** "honest review: [produk] after 2 weeks use..."
- **Curiosity hook:** "kok bisa [produk murah] ngalahin [produk mahal]?"
- **Problem → Solution:** ceritain journey dari susah → nemu solusi

### Format yang GAGAL (HARD SELL)
- ❌ "BELI SEKARANG DISINI LINKNYA!!!"
- ❌ "DISKON 50% HARI INI SAJA!"
- ❌ "Produk ini sangat bagus dan saya merekomendasikannya"
- ❌ Spamming link tanpa konteks

### Link Placement Strategy
- **For COMMENTS (v1-v3):** Link inline in comment text — single line with text, no newline before link (Lexical drops content after \\n)
- **For ORIGINAL POSTS (v4 — THREAD FORMAT ⭐):** Create 3-post thread via "Tambahkan ke utas":
  - POST 1: Hook (relatable opener, 1-2 sentences)
  - POST 2: Review/content (detailed experience, 2-3 sentences)
  - POST 3: Affiliate link + CTA (s.shopee.co.id/XXX + call to action)
  - Link in POST 3 becomes clickable — Threads auto-links URLs
  - "Lihat X lainnya" on profile shows expanded thread with visible link
- **For SINGLE POSTS (v4 fallback):** Threads doesn't allow clickable links in single posts — mention "link di profil" instead
- Jangan spam — ratio 80% konten organic : 20% promosi
- **Cron schedule:** Reply job every 30m (nonstop); Post job 1x per golden hour (cron `30 7,11,19 * * *`)

### Engagement Boosting
- Akhiri dengan pertanyaan ("kalian pernah coba gak?")
- Reply ke trending topics
- Use listicle format
- Bikin "hot take" yang bikin orang comment
- Thread reply sendiri untuk nambah depth

### Frequency
- Sweet spot: 2-3 posts per hari
- Batching: buat 5-7 konten sekaligus, schedule
- Interaksi 15-30 menit sebelum & sesudah posting
- Reply comment orang lain untuk naikin visibility
- Max 1 comment per thread (avoid spam detection)

## 📊 Threads vs Other Platforms

### Threads Characteristics
- More casual than Twitter/X
- Similar to Twitter but with Instagram's social graph
- Algorithm less strict than Instagram
- Text-first (unlike Instagram which is visual-first)
- Integrated with Instagram (same followers)
- Growing Indonesian user base

### Content That Works Better on Threads vs Instagram
- ✅ Opinions, hot takes, personal stories
- ✅ Product recommendations (organic, not ad-looking)
- ✅ Relatable daily life observations
- ✅ Q&A / asking for advice
- ❌ Formal brand announcements
- ❌ Link-heavy posts without context
- ❌ Visual-only content (better on IG)

## 👥 Threads User Behavior (Indonesian Gen Z)

### Posting Time Patterns
- **Lunch break (11:00-13:00):** Users scroll during lunch, high engagement
- **Bedtime scroll (19:00-21:00):** Peak activity before sleep
- **Mid-week peak (Rabu):** Most active day — users post more, scroll more
- **Weekend:** Sabtu 07:00-12:00 (morning scroll), Minggu 19:00-21:00 (evening peak)

### Writing Style Characteristics
- **Short paragraphs:** 1-3 sentences per paragraph, white space between
- **Storytime format:** "jadi gini..." opening, personal narrative flow
- **Hot takes:** "unpopular opinion: ..." or "justru menurut gw..."
- **Relatable hooks:** "siapa yang [situation]?" or "am i the only one"
- **Honest/raw tone:** No filters, no formal language, casual diary-like
- **Question endings:** "kalian gmn?" or "ada yang sama gak?" to boost comments

### Gen Z Abbreviation Patterns
- **Word shortening:** bgt, udh, blm, ntar, skg, kmrn, lg, dr, utk, sm
- **Emphasis doubling:** bgtt (banget banget), bestiee, gilaaa
- **Hybrid slang:** gaje (gak jelas), gws (get well soon), auto (automatically)
- **Emoji as punctuation:** 💀 (emphasis), 😭 (strong emotion), 🤌 (chef's kiss), 💅 (slay), ✨ (sparkle/aesthetic)
- **English loanwords:** no cap, slay, ate, vibe, era, core, NPC, sigma
- **Sentence connectors:** sih, dong, deh, kok, kan, ya — softeners
- **Negation:** ga/gak/gk/engga — all acceptable, ga is fastest to type
- **Affirmation:** gas/gaskeun = let's go, mantul = mantap betul
