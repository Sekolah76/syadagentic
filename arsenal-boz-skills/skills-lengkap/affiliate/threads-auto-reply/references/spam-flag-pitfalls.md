# Threads Reply Spam Pitfalls

## 1. NEVER Put Affiliate Links in Replies
Meta's spam detection aggressively flags accounts that post high volumes of links in replies to other users' threads. This results in comment removal and account suspension (e.g., "Kami menghapus komentar Anda karena spam").
**Solution:** Affiliate links must ONLY be used in top-level original posts (via `threads-auto-post`). For replies, focus purely on engagement. If promoting a product, mention the product name and direct the user to check your profile/DM for the link. **Zero links in replies.**
