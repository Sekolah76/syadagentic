# Threads Auto-Reply: Spam Flag Risks

Meta aggressively flags accounts that drop affiliate URLs directly in replies to other users' threads. This is detected as high-volume spam behavior and will result in comment deletion or account restriction.

**Safety Rules:**
1. **Zero Links in Replies:** Never include `s.shopee.co.id`, `linktr.ee`, or any direct affiliate links in reply comments.
2. **Engagement Only:** Replies should be pure engagement, storytelling, or value-add.
3. **Redirect to Profile:** If promoting a product in a reply, mention the product name and direct the user to check the link in your main profile bio or your own pinned posts.
