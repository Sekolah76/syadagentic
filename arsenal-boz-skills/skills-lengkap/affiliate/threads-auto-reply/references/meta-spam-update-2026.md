# Meta Spam Filters (June 2026 Update)

## Affiliate Links in Replies = High Risk
Meta's spam detection algorithm (Threads/Instagram) aggressively flags accounts that post high volumes of links in replies to other users' posts. 

**Incident:** The `jagonya_shopee` account was flagged for "high volume actions that may disturb others" specifically because of Shopee affiliate links in replies. The flag resulted in comment removal and a temporary restriction, even with human-like delays and rotation. The scan is asynchronous (flagged 2 days after the actual posting).

## Safe Strategy
- **Original Posts:** Affiliate links in original thread posts are generally safe and permissible.
- **Replies:** ZERO links. Replies must be purely organic engagement, validations, or storytelling. If a user asks for a link, direct them to the profile/bio link or send a DM. 
- **Do NOT** attempt to bypass this with URL shorteners in replies; the heuristic targets the behavior of replying with external links to non-followers at scale.
