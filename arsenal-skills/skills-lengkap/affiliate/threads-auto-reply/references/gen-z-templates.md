## 🧠 CONVERSION PSYCHOLOGY FOR REPLIES

### Reply Must Hit 3/5 of These Triggers:
1. **Relatability** — "ini gue banget" → mereka klik karena ngerasa sama
2. **Specificity** — "baru 2 minggu", "udah botol ke 3" → lebih credible
3. **Social Proof** — "500+ sold", "viral" → FOMO
4. **Price Anchor** — "harga under 50rb", "murah meriah" → reduce friction
5. **Personal Experience** — "gw pake", "hasilnya" → authentic

### Reply Structure (MANDATORY)
```
[1 sentence personal experience or relatable statement]
[product mention + specific detail OR result]
[affiliate link] (inline, no newline!)
```

**MAX 2 sentences + link.** Never 3 sentences.

## ❌ ANTI-PATTERNS (NEVER DO)

- ❌ Too formal: "Produk ini sangat bagus dan saya merekomendasikannya"
- ❌ Too salesy: "DISKON 50%! BELI SEKARANG!"
- ❌ Too long: More than 2 sentences + link
- ❌ Forced slang: "Wah bestie, produk ini sungguh luar biasa sekali"
- ❌ Same comment every time: ROTATE patterns!
- ❌ Newline between text and link: Lexical drops content after \n
- ❌ Copy-paste exact same comment across multiple threads
- ❌ Parenthetical text: "(mantap ya)" or "(recommended banget)"
- ❌ Multiple exclamation marks: "BAGUSSS!!! BUY NOW!!!"
- ❌ No link in comment: MANDATORY affiliate link
- ❌ Mentioning "shopee" or "affiliate" explicitly: sounds scammy

## 🎯 CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION TIPS

### What Drives Clicks (based on data):
1. **Price mention** — "under 50rb", "murah meriah" → reduces perceived risk
2. **Specific result** — "dalam 2 minggu", "udah 3x repurchase" → proof it works
3. **Scarcity** — "sering sold out", "coba sebelum habis" → urgency
4. **Social proof** — "500+ sold", "viral di TikTok" → FOMO
5. **Personal experience** — "gw pake", "hasilnya" → trust

### Reply Timing:
- Reply ke posts yang **< 24 jam** (fresh, more visible)
- Reply ke posts yang **< 10 replies** (less competition)
- Reply ke posts yang **punya engagement** (visible to more people)

### Link Placement:
- **ALWAYS inline** — "text text text https://s.shopee.co.id/XXXX text"
- **NEVER newline** — Threads may strip/break links on new lines
- **1 link per comment** — no more, no less
- **Natural context** — don't just paste link at the end
